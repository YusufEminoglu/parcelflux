about=ParcelFlux automates the subdivision of zoning blocks (imar adalar) into parcels with natural variation. It supports single/double-row detection, row-width asymmetry for different front/rear facades, fishbone-style organic boundaries, and automatic merging of residual fragments. Developed with feedback from educational workflows at Dokuz Eylul University, Department of City and Regional Planning.
